ESP32 connection & data format

Open the downloaded HTML in desktop Chrome / Edge, or use localhost. Close Arduino Serial Monitor first. Click Connect ESP32 and choose its USB port. USB serial may be blocked inside this embedded preview.

Send one JSON object per line at the selected baud rate:

{"temperature":32.4,"current":2.5}

Temperature in °C; current in A. Data must come from your calibrated sensors. No GPIO wiring assumptions are made. Reconnect or restart demo to clear the data history. After 5 seconds without valid data, the demand is marked unavailable.
